About Us

Cooling specialists serving Los Angeles since 1997

We supply high-quality radiators and condensers to repair shops, body shops and dealerships, with premium lines like Koyorad and our exclusive KATO Precision Cooling at competitive wholesale prices.

Our story

Pacific Radiators started in 1997 as a small radiator counter in the San Gabriel Valley with one delivery van and a simple promise: the right part, at a fair price, on your lift today.

Over the years we grew into a full cooling-system distributor with two Los Angeles-area warehouses, our own delivery fleet and nationwide shipping. We became an official Koyorad distributor and launched KATO Precision Cooling, our exclusive premium line built to tighter specs for the shops that demand it.

We're still family-owned, and the people who answer the phone still know radiators. That's the difference.
