Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 1, 2026

Pacific Radiators & Auto Parts ("we", "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect when you use our website, call or text us, or buy from us, and how we use it.

Information we collect

  • Information you give us: name, shop name, phone number, email address, vehicle details and anything you include in a quote request, text message or order.
  • Information collected automatically: standard web server logs such as IP address, browser type, pages visited and referring URL. Our site does not use advertising trackers.
  • Payment information: handled by our payment processor at the counter or by phone. We do not store full card numbers on our website.

How we use it

  • To provide quotes, process orders, arrange delivery and handle warranty claims.
  • To respond to your questions by phone, text or email.
  • To maintain trade accounts and invoicing.
  • To improve our website and services.

Text messaging

If you text us or opt in to text updates, we will use your number only to reply to your request, confirm orders and send delivery updates. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP at any time to opt out.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information for money. We share information only with service providers who help us run the business (delivery carriers, payment processors, accounting software) and when required by law.

Cookies

Our website uses only essential cookies needed for it to function. You can disable cookies in your browser without affecting your ability to request a quote.

Your California privacy rights

If you are a California resident, the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) gives you the right to know what personal information we collect, to request its deletion, to correct it, and to not be discriminated against for exercising these rights. To make a request, email privacy@pacificradiators.example or call (626) 555-0147.

Data retention

We keep order and invoice records as long as required for tax and warranty purposes. Quote requests that do not become orders are deleted within 12 months.

Children

Our services are for businesses and adults. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 16.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.

Contact

Questions about this policy: privacy@pacificradiators.example.
